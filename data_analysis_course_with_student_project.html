01 / THE BASICS

What is Data Analysis?

You don't need to be a programmer to understand Data Analysis. You already use its basic idea in everyday life.

Let's understand it simply.

Data means information. Analysis means examining something carefully.

Data Analysis is the process of collecting, organizing and studying information to discover useful answers and make better decisions.

Simple example

Before buying a mobile phone, you compare prices, battery life, camera quality and customer reviews. You study information before making a decision. That is a basic example of data analysis.

Raw Data

Unorganized information such as bills, marks, customer details or sales records.

Analysis

Examining information, comparing values and finding patterns.

Useful Insights

Understanding the results and using them to make informed decisions.

02 / THE PROCESS

How Does Data Analysis Work?

Let's imagine a stationery shop wants to understand its sales performance.

01

Collect Data

Gather information from sales bills, customer orders or daily business records.

02

Clean Data

Correct errors, organize records and check for missing or duplicate information.

03

Analyze Data

Calculate totals, compare products and identify useful patterns.

04

Present Results

Create charts, reports and dashboards to explain what the information tells us.

Remember: The purpose is not just to calculate numbers. It is to understand what those numbers mean.

04 / TOOLS & TECHNOLOGY

Tools You Will Learn.

Each tool has a different purpose. Together, they help us complete the data analysis process.

Microsoft Excel

Organize information in rows and columns, calculate totals and create charts.

Example: Monthly expense calculations.

Python

A programming language used to automate tasks and analyze larger datasets.

Example: Automatically calculate thousands of sales records.

NumPy

A Python library used for numerical calculations and working with arrays of numbers.

Example: Calculate average student marks.

Pandas

Organize, filter, clean and analyze data in table format.

Example: Find customers who spent more than ₹5,000.

Matplotlib

Create graphs and charts to understand patterns in information.

Example: Compare monthly sales using a bar chart.

SQL & MySQL

Store information in databases and retrieve specific records using SQL queries.

Example: Find customer orders from a database.

Power BI

Build interactive dashboards and reports to communicate business information.

Example: View sales performance on one dashboard.

No previous knowledge of these tools is required. The course introduces them step by step.

07 / CAREER SCOPE

Where is Data Analysis Used?

Data is useful in almost every industry. Organizations use it to understand performance and plan ahead.

Banking & Finance

Transaction analysis, spending patterns and financial reports.

Healthcare

Patient appointments, waiting times and hospital operations.

Retail & E-commerce

Product sales, customer preferences and demand trends.

Education

Student attendance, marks and course performance.

Marketing

Campaign performance, website visits and customer engagement.

Logistics

Delivery times, transport costs and order volumes.
